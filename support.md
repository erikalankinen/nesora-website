# Nesora – Support

**Need help with Nesora?**

We're here to help with questions about savings goals, budgeting, data exports, and Nesora Pro.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is my financial data stored?

Nesora stores your financial records locally on your device. Automatic cloud synchronization is not currently available.

### How do I back up my data?

Open **Settings → Data & Backup** and choose **Export Backup**.

Keep the exported file somewhere secure.

### How do I restore my data?

Open **Settings → Data & Backup** and choose **Import Backup**.

Please note that restoring a backup replaces the current financial data in the app.

### How do I cancel Nesora Pro?

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Nesora Pro screen and select **Restore Purchases**.

## Contact Support

For questions, bug reports, or assistance, email:

**nesora.app@gmail.com**

Please include a description of the issue and, if relevant, your iOS version.

Do not send financial records, passwords, payment card information, or other sensitive information in support emails.
