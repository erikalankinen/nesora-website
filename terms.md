
# Nesora – Terms of Use

**Last updated: November 5, 2026**

## 1. About Nesora

Nesora is a personal savings and budgeting application.

Users can manually track financial information, organize budgets, and monitor their savings goals.

Nesora does not provide banking services, investment brokerage services, or personalized financial advice.

## 2. Free and Pro Features

Nesora offers both free and optional Pro features.

The free version includes up to two savings goals, basic budgeting, transaction recording, and total savings tracking.

Nesora Pro provides access to additional features, including unlimited savings goals, advanced charts, extended history, savings forecasts, supported customization options, and CSV/PDF exports.

## 3. Subscriptions

Nesora Pro is available through monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions.

Subscription prices are displayed within the app before purchase and are processed by Apple.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled according to Apple's subscription terms.

Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through their Apple Account subscription settings.

Canceling renewal does not normally remove access before the current paid subscription period ends.

When Pro access ends, previously recorded financial data is retained, but Pro-only functionality may become unavailable.

## 4. Financial Information

Nesora is intended for personal financial organization.

All calculations, savings forecasts, and visualizations are provided for informational purposes only.

Forecasts are estimates based on recorded information and do not guarantee future financial results.

Users are responsible for reviewing the accuracy of the information they enter.

## 5. Data and Backups

Nesora stores financial information locally on the user's device.

Users are encouraged to maintain their own backups using the provided export functionality.
