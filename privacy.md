# Nesora – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 5, 2026**

Nesora respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Nesora mobile application handles information.

## 1. Financial Information

Nesora allows users to manually record savings goals, financial transactions, budgets, and related information.

Financial records are stored locally on the user's device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Nesora does not require users to create an account or connect a bank account.

## 2. Data Collection

The current version of Nesora does not send users' manually entered financial records to a developer-operated server.

Nesora does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

## 3. Subscriptions

Nesora offers optional Pro subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Subscription purchases and payments are processed by Apple using StoreKit. Nesora uses subscription status information to determine access to Pro features.

Nesora does not directly collect or store payment card details.

Apple processes information according to its own privacy policies.

## 4. Data Storage and Backups

Financial records are stored locally within the app.

Nesora does not currently provide automatic synchronization between devices.

Users can export their data using the app's backup and export features.

Exported JSON, CSV, and PDF files are not additionally encrypted by Nesora. Users are responsible for choosing secure storage locations for exported files.

Files saved to iCloud Drive or another cloud provider may be processed by that provider under its privacy policy.

Device-level backups may also be managed through Apple's operating system and account settings.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can edit or delete supported financial records within the application.

Uninstalling Nesora removes the app's local data from the device, subject to any separate device backups or exported copies.

Exported files must be deleted separately from wherever the user has saved them.

## 6. Data Security

Nesora relies on Apple's platform security mechanisms for local application storage.

No software can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices and store exported financial files securely.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Nesora's functionality or data practices change.

The latest version will be made available on this page.
